Indian games

       

  • Cricket - The most popular sport in India, governed by the BCCI. India has won multiple ICC trophies, including the World Cup.
  • Hockey - India's national sport. The men's team has won multiple Olympic gold medals.
  • Football - The Indian Super League (ISL) and I-League are the top football leagues in India.
  • Kabaddi - A traditional Indian sport. India has dominated in international competitions like the Kabaddi World Cup.
  • Badminton - India has produced world-class players like PV Sindhu and Saina Nehwal.
  • Wrestling - Indian wrestlers like Sushil Kumar and Bajrang Punia have won Olympic medals.
  • Boxing - Mary Kom and Vijender Singh are among India's most successful boxers.
  • Chess - India is the birthplace of chess. Grandmasters like Viswanathan Anand have made India proud.
  • Athletics - Neeraj Chopra won India's first Olympic gold medal in javelin throw.
  • Table Tennis - India has rising stars in TT, with players competing globally.
  • Archery - India has world-class archers like Deepika Kumari.
  • Shooting - Indian shooters like Abhinav Bindra and Manu Bhaker have won medals in Olympics and CWG.
  • Swimming - The sport is growing in India with participation in international events.
  • Weightlifting - India has produced champions like Mirabai Chanu.
  • Tennis - India has had success in doubles, with players like Leander Paes and Sania Mirza.
  • Kho Kho - A traditional Indian tag sport played in rural areas.
  • Gilli Danda - An ancient Indian game similar to cricket and baseball.
  • Mallakhamb - A traditional sport involving gymnastic movements on a wooden pole.
  • Cycling - Indian cyclists are competing at the international level.
  • Marathon Running - Long-distance running events are gaining popularity.
  • Ice Hockey - Played in regions like Ladakh where ice rinks are available.
  • Formula Racing - India hosted the Formula 1 Indian Grand Prix.
  • Motorcycle Racing - The MotoGP event in India is growing in popularity.
  • Rugby - The sport is growing in India with participation in Asian tournaments.
  • Basketball - India competes in international basketball events.
  • Volleyball - Played in schools and colleges across the country.
  • Throwball - A lesser-known sport mainly played in South India.
  • Rowing - India competes in international rowing events.
  • Yachting - Popular in coastal regions.
  • Golf - Indian golfers like Anirban Lahiri have competed in global tournaments.
  • Powerlifting - India has strong representation in powerlifting competitions.
  • Paragliding - Popular in adventure sports destinations like Himachal Pradesh.
  • Rock Climbing - Adventure sport gaining popularity.
  • Polo - A traditional sport played in royal circles in India.
  • Equestrian - India competes in equestrian events.
  • Skiing - Limited to regions like Gulmarg in Jammu & Kashmir.
  • Sepak Takraw - A Southeast Asian sport gaining popularity in India.
  • Handball - India has a national team participating in international events.
  • Softball - A niche sport in India.
  • Fencing - India has started competing in international fencing events.
  • Horse Racing - Popular in cities like Mumbai and Bangalore.
  • Ultimate Frisbee - A new-age sport growing among youth.
  • Kalaripayattu - One of the oldest martial arts from Kerala.
  • Judo - Indian judokas participate in the Olympics.
  • Taekwondo - India is improving in this martial art.
  • Karate - Karate has a strong following in India.
  • Muay Thai - A lesser-known combat sport in India.
  • Mixed Martial Arts (MMA) - Gaining popularity due to UFC events.
