    About:

    The Quiz Game is an interactive, engaging web application where players answer a series of multiple-choice questions within a given time limit. Each correct answer earns points, and players are awarded additional points based on how quickly they answer. The game is divided into various categories and difficulty levels, ensuring there's something for everyone. At the end of the quiz, the player's total score is displayed, showing their rank compared to others. The goal is to answer as many questions correctly and quickly as possible to top the leaderboard.

    How TO Play:

  • Start the Quiz: Click on the "Start Quiz" button to begin the game.
  • Answer Questions: You’ll be presented with multiple-choice questions. Choose the correct answer from the given options.
  • Time Limit: Each question has a time limit. Answer as quickly as possible to earn more points.
  • Complete the Quiz: After answering all the questions, your total score will be displayed.
